# Epochal Capital

**AI-Powered Investment Platform for Private AI Companies with Liquidity Potential**

---

## What is Epochal Capital?

Epochal Capital is a multi-agent investment platform designed to identify, evaluate, and invest in private AI companies approaching liquidity events (IPO, M&A, secondary transactions).

The platform combines:

- **Automated Deal Sourcing** — Continuous scanning of 22+ private AI companies
- **Quantitative Thesis Scoring** — Objective evaluation against configurable criteria
- **Research Automation** — Twice-weekly intelligence refresh across all tracked companies
- **Portfolio Management** — Track positions across direct investments, SPVs, and funds
- **Investor Relations** — Automated content generation for LP communications

## Why Multi-Agent?

Traditional investment workflows are manual, inconsistent, and limited by human bandwidth. Epochal Capital uses specialized AI agents that:

1. **Work 24/7** — Continuous monitoring without fatigue
2. **Scale effortlessly** — Add new companies without proportional effort
3. **Maintain consistency** — Same scoring methodology applied uniformly
4. **Adapt quickly** — Update thesis criteria and see immediate impact
